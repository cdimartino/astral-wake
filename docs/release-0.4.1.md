# Astral Wake 0.4.1 — The Sound of the Void

- The opening keeps a quiet breath through the title handoff, with one distant glass call. The star chart has a deeper, wider ambience even before you jump.
- Rewards, shops, and recovery share a quiet cabin ambience. Crossing into a new sector has a sustained warp sound with stereo echoes rather than a series of separate cues.
- Enemy attacks sound distinct from ward. A kill ends with a short hull collapse; clearing an encounter adds a quieter, brief minor-toned response.
- Voyage victory is a widening hull resonance without a fanfare. Defeat keeps its descending phrase, at about half the previous length, with a spacious undertone.
- The disliked low bowed sound has been replaced across the factory bank. The distinct knock-and-string response for the Glass Beyond guardian is unchanged.

This update retains the voyage, combat controls, save format, and balance of 0.4.0. The public app uses the regular **Astral Wake** save folder; the separately installed **Audio Preview** uses its own.

The macOS ZIP includes Apple Silicon and Intel binaries and is ad-hoc signed, not notarized. See the [installation guide](https://github.com/cdimartino/astral-wake#install-on-macos).
