## Final constrained offers

3,000 offers per row/generation: 100 successive offers in each of 30 seed families. Ordinary=3 cards, ordinary Pockets=4, elite=2, elite Pockets=3. IDs are without replacement inside an offer, restored for the next offer. Sparse=no rares, first two eligible common IDs, and at most two visible cards.

Final percentages are **observations, not nominal probabilities**: rarity exhaustion, final-slot primary diversity, and first-slot pity change them. Pockets is separate, not pooled. Elite rows isolate card generation: they do not interleave the separate powerup shuffle or other encounters. Bosses currently use those same card weights but retain their existing flow pending #20.

## Reproduce

```sh
"$GODOT_BIN" --headless --path . --script tests/reward_distribution_test.gd -- --report=docs/baselines/reward-distribution-v1.json
python3 tools/reward_report.py docs/baselines/reward-distribution-v1.json > docs/reward-distribution-report.md
```

Repeated identical runs must produce byte-identical JSON; source hashes make changed candidates distinguishable. Native demo instructions and policy limits: [distribution design](reward-distribution.md).
