# Enemy field guide

Enemy cycles are fixed and restart at the first step in each encounter. Each phase's stance applies **while its intent is showing**, before the enemy acts. Interrupting skips that action, not the next step in the cycle. Hover or focus the intent badge for exact values and the complete repeating pattern.

Strain chooses the busiest organs (ties: organ index); cuts follow the last command's conduits before circuit order. Neither targeting rule rolls randomly. Group composition and action order still matter: a strain enemy can set up an overload attacker, and a medic can keep an armored partner alive.

## Cradle of Embers

| Enemy | What to learn |
| --- | --- |
| Cinder Mite | Bite → Soot. Its strain turn is exposed: use that window for multiple hits. |
| Lantern Eel | Arc → Afterglow. Defend the armored Arc, burst into the exposed Afterglow. |
| Ash Cantor | Hymn → Cough. Interrupt the hymn or vent before saturation strengthens Cough. |
| Ribbon Leech | Unstitch → Drink. Short routes exploit its weakness and avoid its cuts; prevention denies feeding. |
| Kiln Shepherd | Brand → Kiln breath → Crook. Armor first, interruptible setup second, exposed overload strike last. |
| Unhatched Sun | Egg → Corona → Fever → Shellfall. Interrupt laying or kill eggs; burst Corona, cool before Shellfall. |

Ember Cocoons do not attack and hatch after two enemy phases. Disruption cannot delay them. Newborn Flares attack every turn but have low hull: kill them quickly or catch them in split damage.

## Crown of Storms

| Enemy | What to learn |
| --- | --- |
| Spark Knight | Challenge → Riposte. Long routes exploit its weakness, but saturation empowers its exposed counterstrike. Burst without overheating. |
| Corona Wisp | Static → Arc → Return stroke. Interrupt its fragile setup or cool before overload. The last strike is exposed too. |
| Storm Kite | Snip → Thunderline. Bait the armored cut, preserve cooling for overload, and exploit its secondary-split weakness. |
| Arc Herald | Proclamation → Oath → Echoing oath. Armor, then exposed interruptible mark, then drain. Spend prevention on the last turn. |
| Thunder Regent | Scepter → Divide → Judgment. Interrupt the double cut; cool before, then burst into, exposed overload Judgment. |
| Crown Unbound | Shatter crown → Royal thunder → Coronation → Unbound arc. Interrupt cuts or strain, burst the exposed royal strike, and keep capacity free before the overload finale. |
