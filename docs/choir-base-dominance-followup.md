# Required follow-up: Choir base dominance before new cards

**Open design prerequisite for any future Choir card expansion. Not resolved by
#19 distribution, and not permission to rebalance bases in this candidate.**

`bright_canon` and `crescendo` both cost **2 CP**, are attacks, print Concert,
and have no base healing, draw, cooling, disruption, resource gain or link.
Bright Canon prints **12 damage**, Crescendo only **10**; their Concert arithmetic
is identical. At base level, with the same organs/sequence/resources, Bright Canon
is strictly superior in that damage channel without an offsetting printed cost.
It is also common versus Crescendo's uncommon. Distinct authored branch identities
and Crescendo's restorative evolution do not erase that base-level comparison.

For #19 **only**, new-shop Bright Canon's price has an explicit 40-salvage floor,
equal to Crescendo, rather than the ordinary common 30. This prevents a cheaper
strict base upgrade but **does not solve the card design problem**. Old generation-0
shops keep their exact prices. Neither rarity nor weight hides/rebalances a base.

Before authoring more Choir near-duplicates:

1. Decide an explicit, mechanically real cost/payload/role distinction; compare
   both cards at zero and positive Concert spend, kind-repeat reset, full hull,
   entry strain and exact kill/prevention thresholds.
2. Audit all same-CP same-kind base channels and cheap repair/generation options
   (including Full/Light Resonant Shell) instead of assuming higher rarity helps.
3. Review both named branches and legacy copies separately. Do not erase existing
   saved identities or silently change old run/distribution policy.
4. Require paired tactical cases and a fresh manual expedition on the proposed
   rebalance. Remove/adjust the temporary price floor only with an explicit design
   decision and a new version/compatibility plan where necessary.

No stats, base costs, branches or unlocks are changed here. This follow-up is not
a claim that every rare should dominate, nor that a deck must acquire a Concert.
