# Beta feedback form (Q7, wider beta)

Purpose: collect comprehension and fit evidence from players outside the comprehension sessions. Use after a build is shared (the owner exports and distributes the build; that step is manual).

Hosting: any form tool the owner chooses. The questions below are the content; keep the order.

## Before you start

Thanks for testing Astral Wake. This takes about ten minutes. Please play at least one full battle before answering the questions in Part 2. Nothing you enter identifies you unless you give contact details at the end.

## Part 1: Background

1. Have you played deck-building or roguelike games before? (Never / A few / Many)
2. Which ship did you play? (Cartographer / Palimpsest / Hollow Choir / Not sure)
3. About how long did your first battle take, in minutes?

## Part 2: Understanding

Answer in your own words. Skip anything you don't remember.

4. In one sentence, what does **Disruption** do?
5. What does an organ's **Strain** do?
6. What did you think a route was, and how did you choose one?
7. Which organ or term confused you most? Describe what you tried.
8. Did you open the guide or any tooltip during play? (Never / Once / Several times / Constantly)

## Part 3: Routing and fit

9. How clear was the link between an organ and what it did to your card? (1 = not clear, 5 = very clear)
10. How many organs did you feel you had to learn to play well? (A few / Some / Too many)
11. Would you prefer a smaller starting set of organs that grow during a run? (Yes / No / Not sure) Why?
12. Did routing feel like a meaningful choice? (1 = no, 5 = yes)

## Part 4: Open feedback

13. What would you change first?
14. Anything that surprised you, good or bad?

## Part 5: Contact (optional)

15. If you'd like to be contacted about a follow-up session, leave an email or handle. This is stored separately from your answers.

## Analysis plan (for the owner)

- Score Part 2 questions 4 and 5 against the same rubric used in `comprehension-check.md` (R1, R2).
- Count Q7 mentions by term. Compare with the term audit (`term-audit.md`).
- Q10 and Q11 test H2 and the start-size direction. Treat as preference evidence, not a measurement.
- Store responses outside the repository.
