# Astral Wake 0.4.0 — Chart a Voyage

- Plot a course through a branching star chart. Inspect a beacon without traveling; choose **Travel** to jump. You can see the risk or service, but not the enemies waiting at the destination. A navigation soundscape accompanies the jump; Reduced Motion keeps the chart still.
- Drag a command through connected organs. Releasing an attack arms it; click a living enemy to fire. A self-only command resolves on release. Click and keyboard routing remain available.
- Right-click a card or organ, use **Details**, or press **I** while focused to inspect. In combat, details sit beside the fight. **Esc** closes them without clearing your staged route.
- Defeated enemies fade with a visible cue and a short musical response. The last foe triggers a victory flourish before rewards. Simultaneous kills share one sound.
- Reward and shop buttons line up at the bottom of their cards. **Details** neither takes the reward nor buys the item.

The macOS download includes Apple Silicon and Intel binaries. It is ad-hoc signed, not notarized. Extract the ZIP to use the app; see the [installation guide](https://github.com/cdimartino/astral-wake#install-on-macos) for Gatekeeper instructions.
